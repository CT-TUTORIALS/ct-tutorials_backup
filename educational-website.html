3. ICT-Integrated Lesson Plan

The lesson uses the website as a digital teaching and learning environment. Learners can access explanations, teaching aids, participate in an interactive activity and complete the online assessment.

Stage Teacher Activity Learner Activity ICT Integration Assessment
Introduction Introduce the topic and activate learners' prior knowledge. Respond to introductory questions and discuss what they already know. Learners access the website and introductory learning material. Oral questioning.
Content Development Explain the key concepts using the learning material and teaching aids. Read the explanations, observe the diagram and watch the educational video. Website, diagram and video. Questioning and observation.
Learner Activity Guide learners through the interactive task. Work individually or in groups to complete the activity. Interactive website activity. Activity responses.
Assessment Administer the digital quiz. Complete the online assessment. Digital quiz. Quiz score.
Conclusion Summarise the main concepts and address misconceptions. State what they learned and ask final questions. Digital lesson summary. Exit question.

Pedagogical Justification

The website supports learner-centred teaching by allowing learners to interact with learning materials rather than relying exclusively on teacher explanation. The combination of written content, visual teaching aids, video and an interactive assessment provides learners with different ways of engaging with the topic.

6. Support for Different Learning Needs

Adaptation 1: Simplified Instructions

Instructions are presented in short, numbered steps and difficult concepts are explained using simpler language.

Who does this support?
This supports learners who may experience difficulty processing lengthy or complex instructions.

Adaptation 2: Readable Digital Content

The website uses clear headings, readable text, adequate spacing and a responsive layout that can be accessed on different screen sizes.

Who does this support?
This supports learners who benefit from clearly organised and visually accessible learning materials.

Adaptation 3: Alternative Text

Educational images and diagrams include alternative text describing their important information.

Who does this support?
This supports learners who use screen readers or have difficulty accessing visual information.

Adaptation 4: Downloadable Material

Learners can be provided with a downloadable copy of the lesson material for offline revision.

Who does this support?
This supports learners who have limited or intermittent internet access.

7. Digital Citizenship

Responsible Online Participation

Learners should communicate respectfully when using digital platforms. They should not use technology to bully, harass, impersonate or harm other people.

Copyright

Learners and teachers should respect copyright when using digital materials. Images, videos, diagrams and other resources should be properly acknowledged and used according to the relevant permissions or licences.

Learner Privacy

Learners' personal information should not be published on the website without appropriate permission. Learner photographs, contact details and other identifying information should not be publicly displayed.

Privacy reminder: Do not enter real learner names, telephone numbers, addresses, passwords or other private information into this website.
